Climate-smart MPA management

Bridging the gap between climate data and reef management decisions

CliMPA equips Marine Protected Area managers in the Western Indian Ocean with the best available climate data, and a practical framework for putting it to use, starting with the Mafia Island Marine Park (MIMP), Tanzania.

Why this exists

Marine Protected Areas in the Western Indian Ocean are on the frontline of climate change. Rising sea temperatures, shifting ocean chemistry, accelerating sea level rise, and increasingly erratic rainfall are fundamentally altering the ecosystems MPAs are designed to protect, and the livelihoods of the communities that depend on them.

CliMPA is a pilot Research & Development initiative (Oct 2024 – Oct 2026) that bridges this gap by mainstreaming climate data into MPA decision-making, co-creating a practical framework with the biodiversity and MPA managers who will use it.
