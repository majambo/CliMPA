Climate-smart MPA management

From Climate Awareness to Climate-Informed Planning in Marine Protected Areas

Through collaboration between scientists, marine protected area managers, and coastal communities, CliMPA transforms climate knowledge into practical management solutions. First implemented at Mafia Island Marine Park, the initiative serves as a proof of concept for a scalable approach capable of delivering broader conservation and climate adaptation benefits across the Western Indian Ocean.
