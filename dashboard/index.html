CliMPA · WIOMSA SCALABLE Initiative — prototype covering all 158 WIO MPAs across 9 countries (South Africa, Tanzania, Seychelles, Mauritius, Madagascar, France, Kenya, Mozambique, Comoros). Source: Bio-Oracle v3.0 decadal projections, SSP2-4.5 / SSP5-8.5, baseline 2000–2019 (thetao/so) or 2000–2018 (ph/dfe). SST thresholds — 29.5°C Watch (DHW≥4°C-wk) and 32°C Severe (DHW≥8°C-wk) — are the only established management thresholds in the current CliMPA framework; pH, salinity and dissolved iron are shown descriptively pending threshold definition. Dissolved iron units follow the standard Bio-Oracle/CMIP6 "dfe" convention (mol/m³) — not confirmed against source metadata, as the CSV carries no units column. Sea level rise, storms and rainfall are not present in this dataset.
