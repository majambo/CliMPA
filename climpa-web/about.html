The project

About CliMPA

Western Indian Ocean (WIO) Marine Protected Area Climate Futures

"Creating a framework that integrates climate data into the planning and management of Marine Protected Areas (MPAs) for adaptive, resilient conservation in the WIO."
Duration: 30 Oct 2024 – 30 Oct 2026 Contact: climpa.info@gmail.com

Project overview

CliMPA is a pilot Research & Development initiative aimed at integrating climate change considerations into the management of Marine Protected Areas in the Western Indian Ocean. Climate threats affect both the economy and society across WIO nations, and roughly half of the region's MPAs sit in climate-vulnerable areas with significant impacts on coastal communities.

CliMPA seeks to bridge knowledge gaps by mainstreaming climate data into MPA decision-making, for enhanced, resilient management of these vital social-ecological systems.

Aim & objectives

"The primary goal of the project is to equip biodiversity and MPA managers in the WIO region with the best available climate change data, and to co-create a practical framework for incorporating this information into the management of MPAs."

Project site: Mafia Island, Tanzania

Size

822 km²

Ecosystems: coral reefs, mangroves, seagrass, coastal forests. Biodiversity includes endangered species such as green and hawksbill turtles.

Climate change impacts

+0.56°C Sea temperature rise, 2001–2006

Associated with coral bleaching, with recovery supported by active management. Ocean acidification and shifting fish populations are reducing catches and creating economic instability.

Challenges

Overfishing and destructive fishing practices continue to damage coral reefs, heightening the park's climate vulnerability alongside warming and acidification.

Adaptive management needs

Focus: resilience through monitoring and research.
Action: climate-smart interventions to sustain ecosystem services across park zones.

Background & need

  • Climate impact on marine ecosystems: rapid shifts due to climate change affect species distributions, biodiversity, and habitats, especially near the equator. Adaptive management is needed, but significant knowledge gaps remain.
  • Gaps in MPA management: few MPAs integrate climate considerations, risking ineffective conservation under changing conditions.
  • WIO region challenges: climate change endangers the economy and communities across the WIO, where half of MPAs face climate risk, and regional data to inform climate-resilient decisions remains limited.
  • Rationale for CliMPA: the project co-develops climate data and frameworks, enhancing MPA resilience through FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) climate services, bridging scientific data with practical conservation needs.

WP1: Identify Climate & Relevant Data Needs MPRU & Macquarie Define essential climate and socio-ecological data for MPA management. Host 3 regional workshops with stakeholders to identify climate indices and data needs. Integrated workshop report (m12); portfolio of key climate data for MPAs (m14); academic paper (m24)
WP2: Integrate Climate Data in MPA Management TAFIRI & CORDIO Establish pathways for embedding climate data in MPA planning and operational processes. Conduct 2 workshops on climate data integration and scenario planning; develop a framework for MPA climate data integration. Integration framework (m14); academic paper on climate services for MPAs
WP3: Develop Climate Change Factsheet ABC & Macquarie Create a WIO-specific climate factsheet tailored for MPA managers. Compile regional climate data into a user-friendly factsheet. WIO region climate change factsheet for MPA managers (m20)
WP4: Knowledge Transfer & Capacity Building MPRU & Hereon Build capacity for climate-informed MPA management within the WIO. Develop a climate module for MPA manager training; create a prototype platform for climate services. Training material for the WIO-Compass programme (m18); prototype climate services platform (m20)
WP5: Project Management & Communication CORDIO, Hereon & Macquarie Ensure project success through effective management, communication, and visibility. Oversee resource allocation, communication, evaluation, and compliance; organise General Assemblies and disseminate findings. Project reports; assemblies; dissemination products (policy briefs, blogs, videos, papers)

Reporting & dissemination

CliMPA reports to WIOMSA through three Working Group General Assemblies (one online, two in-region), and disseminates its work through a policy brief for the UNEP Nairobi Convention on climate, MPAs, and communities of practice, alongside blog posts, video clips, academic papers, and conference presentations, ten specific outputs in total as set out in the work plan.
